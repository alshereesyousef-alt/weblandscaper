Frequently Asked Questions

When will I get my quote?

We strive to provide a detailed estimate within 24-48 hours of receiving your request. For complex landscaping projects, an on-site visit may be required before finalizing the quote.

Do I need to be home for the estimate?

In most cases, no. As long as we have clear access to the areas requiring service (like an unlocked gate for the backyard), we can assess the property and email you the proposal.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Cutting Edge Lawn Care is fully licensed and carries comprehensive liability insurance to protect your property and provide peace of mind during our service visits.
